SMS Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

This SMS Policy describes how Guild of Opulence™ / Ulrich Kakou Coaching ("we," "us," or "our") uses SMS (text message) communications to interact with individuals who have opted in to receive messages from us.

Program Description

Guild of Opulence™ sends SMS messages to individuals who have voluntarily opted in through our website application form or other opt-in mechanisms. Messages may include:

  • Program information and updates
  • Application status notifications
  • Appointment reminders and scheduling
  • Educational content related to our programs
  • Promotional offers and announcements
  • Follow-up communications related to your inquiry

How to Opt In

You opt in to receive SMS messages by providing your phone number and checking the SMS consent checkbox on our application form at guildofopulence.com. By doing so, you expressly consent to receive text messages from us at the phone number provided.

Consent Language: "By checking this box, I agree to receive SMS text messages from Guild of Opulence™ / Ulrich Kakou Coaching at the phone number provided. Message frequency may vary. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help."

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with our programs and team. You may receive messages at any time, though we aim to communicate during reasonable business hours.

Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Guild of Opulence™ does not charge for SMS messages, but your carrier may.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any SMS message you receive from us
  • Contacting us directly at ulrich@ulrichkakou.com

After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and will no longer receive SMS communications from us, unless you opt back in.

How to Get Help

For assistance, reply HELP to any SMS message, or contact us at:

  • Email: ulrich@ulrichkakou.com
  • Website: guildofopulence.com

Carrier Liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery of SMS messages is subject to effective transmission from your network operator.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your phone number is used solely for the purposes described in this policy. Please review our full Privacy Policy for more information.

Supported Carriers

Our SMS program is supported by most major U.S. carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. Availability may vary by carrier.
